# Kitchen v14 redo — image-generation record

## V2 cooker-door correction

The v1 `cooker-door-v14.png` is retained only as rejected history. The v2 edit
target used that door for broad styling, the fixed cooker body for aperture
alignment, and the approved v8 cooker for line-density calibration. The final
prompt required a door-only silhouette for a 150 × 82 px footprint: cream
enamel frame, horizontal copper handle and empty smoked glass, with no cavity,
racks, shelves, food or glow. A targeted second pass made the silhouette taller
so uniform containment leaves no more than 4 px transparent padding on any
edge. The selected source is `cooker-door-v14-v2.png`.

Tool: OpenAI built-in image generator. Use case: `stylized-concept`, with
`precise-object-edit` only for matching removable doors and burnt contents.

## Shared prompt contract

Every item was requested as a newly painted, isolated 2× raster master in the
approved v8 adult/playful ligne-claire family: confident 3–4 px-equivalent dark
ink at final scale, matt fills, one shadow tone, upper-left internal modelling,
duck-egg painted wood, cream enamel, honey pine and copper accents. The camera
is front-on elevation; depth appears only as a shallow straight-up top reveal.
Every prompt prohibited rooms, floors, cast/contact shadows, gloss, chrome,
magenta, text/logos/watermarks, isometric views and sideways skew. Each brief
named its exact v14 rectangle and required a silhouette that survives the
lighthouse reduction.
