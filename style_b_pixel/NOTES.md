# Style Pixel

Consistent 4 px logical pixel grid, authored at 256 px and scaled by integer factors only. Do not freely rotate sprites; use 15-degree increments or redraw key angles to preserve the grid.

- Light: upper-left; anchor: bottom-centre.
- Keeper target in-game size: 90x130 px, authored at 2x or above.
- Separate moving objects: TV screen, lamp glow, toilet lid, fridge door, piano lid should remain separate in production.
- Animation: preserve generous joint overlap; mirror the keeper horizontally rather than redrawing.
- Keep faces simple; let bubbles, body language and sound carry emotion.
