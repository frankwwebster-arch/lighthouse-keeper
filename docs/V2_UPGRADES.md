# V2 ideas (listed only, not built)

The consolidated expansion, room, weather, transport and energy design is now in `docs/EXPANSION_DESIGN.md`. That document separates locked decisions from the unbuilt idea backlog and supersedes any conflicting floor-order notes here.

- Spendable upgrades: remote-control lamp switch, better cooker (faster, bigger meals), games console (fun), better bed (energy), second TV channel pack, bookshelf refills.
- Food tiers: premium ingredients and meals he especially likes.
- Mini games: fishing (timing), cooking (recipe steps), gardening (watering rhythm), piano (typing notes).
- Floors unlock through varied missions and player choice; standard-floor progression is non-linear and avoids repetitive grind.
- Early shop unlock: the shop is off-island and belongs to its proprietor, so it never breaks as a keeper asset. Replace the temporary on-island fallback with a proper trip from the keeper's jetty.
- Upgradeable keeper boat for shop and later sea travel: rowing boat → tug → speedboat. The jetty and boat are keeper-owned, repairable assets.
- Workshop floor: reduces breakdown pressure (frequency/chance), but does not make the lighthouse permanently fault-proof. Its effectiveness should be data-driven and visible to the grown-ups' breakdown dials.
- More visitors with stories; seasons and holidays; birthdays.
- Floor-by-floor camera mode.
- Sounds and music.
