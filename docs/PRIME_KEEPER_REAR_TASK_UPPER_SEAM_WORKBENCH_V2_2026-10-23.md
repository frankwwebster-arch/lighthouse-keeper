## Corrected diagnosis

The unstable join in rear-task frames 11–31 is the moving **upper seam of the
ribbed jumper band**, not the lower seam between that band and the trousers.
The approved rear Prime authority already contains the correct complete ribbed
band, lower seam, trouser modelling and boots. V2 therefore preserves those
parts together as one exact lower layer.

The earlier lower-seam experiment remains available at
`/prime-keeper-rear-task-alignment` as reversible V1 evidence. V2 has its own
browser-storage key and asset query prefix; it does not overwrite V1 settings,
source art or review exports.

## V2 compositing contract

- The user traces immediately above the faulty moving upper seam with the same
  five independently editable points and 4× waist view.
- The crop boundary is not given a black outline. Doing so would recreate the
  seam being removed.
- Any small gap down to the approved band is filled column-by-column from clean
  pixels in the moving jumper body, retaining its horizontal colour variation.
- Approved source row 201 onward supplies the complete upper band seam, ribbing,
  lower band seam, trousers and boots. The narrow ownership mask excludes the
  authority's hands.
- The exact approved Prime ghost remains a separate 10–80% reference layer and
  never enters measurements, JSON or PNG exports.
- All 21 frame settings remain independent and can be reset or re-drafted.
