## Frank's changes to your brief

1. **No cloud by default.** Frank shared a comic panel as a reference:
   symbols fanned out over a character's head. In the sketch, pictures flick
   out from the Keeper's head and hang in an arc above it, each tilted
   outward along its spoke, with small spinning spiral swirls between them
   and teardrop flicks shooting out from the head. Draw all of this in our
   own style; do not copy the reference. Your cloud puff stays as the
   alternative. With no bubble, the callout can never be mistaken for the
   future wants bubble.
2. **Furniture behind him, not in front.** The counter, sink, basin, planter
   or bench stands against the back wall *behind* the Keeper, and he stands
   in front of it facing away from us. His own back hides his hands, and
   nothing stands in front of him. Layer order: room, furniture, Keeper,
   callout. This replaces the foreground-occluder order in your brief's
   section 5.

## The phrase in the sketch (5.2 s, then it repeats)

- 0.00 to 0.35 s: swirls and flicks (or the cloud) swell in.
- 0.35 s: the main picture pops in at the top of the arc with a burst of
  short lines, then bobs in time with his shoulders.
- From 0.9 s: helpers flick out one at a time onto their spokes, jiggle,
  then shrink away outward.
- 3.95 to 4.6 s: the main picture and the envelope leave.
- 4.6 to 5.2 s: a quiet beat, then the next phrase picks different helpers.
- Still version (reduced motion): the envelope, the main picture and one
  helper, with no movement; the helper changes each phrase.

The timings are a starting point; tune them at real size on an iPad.
