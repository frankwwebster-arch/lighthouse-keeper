# Kitchen V12 plant-shelf-standard recipe shelf

Date: 17 October 2026  
Status: review draft; not approved, promoted or wired

V11 is rejected because its independently scaled shelf was thinner and smaller
than the established plant shelf. V12 uses the V10 plant shelf as its structural
authority and changes only the contents: recipe books between two jar bookends.

- Exact cell: `21 × 56 lp`; opaque width: `19 lp`.
- Bottom: `25 lp` from room top, matching the plant shelf.
- Final raster rows `83..99` (plank and brackets) are copied byte-for-byte from
  `obj_fridge_wall_shelf_v10_f1.png`.
- Contents retain the plant shelf's visual scale; V12 opaque height must be at
  least `9.5 lp`.
- Parent: kettle; no interaction spot.
- Kettle and shelf share exact whole-bay transforms at bays 2, 5 and 8.
- V10 and V11 files remain immutable; nothing is wired into the game.
