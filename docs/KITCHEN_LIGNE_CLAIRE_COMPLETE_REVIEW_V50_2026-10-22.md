## Outcome

V50 is the full movable Kitchen V46 broken-appliance motion workbench with only
the rejected Dishwasher V1 family replaced by geometry-locked Dishwasher V3.
It retains the three exact approved Kettle V47 states, the separate kettle lid
motion, appliance tremor modes, independently anchored effects and replaceable
review SFX from V46. It contains 60 object/state records, 16 effect records and
16 contact/evidence sheets.

V48 is retained as an immutable intermediate: its first build stopped before
archive registration because two inherited routes shared the basename
`manifest.json`; its exact assets and shell were preserved and only the missing
content-addressed route record was completed. V49 fixed that builder fault and
is retained as the immutable immediate predecessor. V50 changes only two
review-copy references so the drawing/ghost link points at Dishwasher V3 and
the review boundary names V50 while explicitly retaining V46. No art or
behaviour differs between V49 and V50.

## Dishwasher geometry and ghost audit

All 15 Off/On/Broken adjacency candidates preserve the corresponding approved
V41 `98 × 82 px` canvas and alpha channel byte-for-byte. This locks the outer
silhouette, transparent padding, placement, door, carcass, perspective,
footprint, anchors, pivots, attachment points and collision envelope.

The V3 evidence includes current/candidate alpha ghosts, native 64 px proof,
one invariant control-panel proof and the complete fitted worktop-run ghost.
The ghost confirms that the dishwasher is not shorter or squatter than V41;
the earlier impression came from a visibly disconnected cap treatment.

## One panel and one worktop run

Off, On and Broken use one physical panel geometry: LCD upper-left, selector
upper-right and tiny status lamp immediately to the selector's right. Only
state indication changes. No lamp or display is transposed onto the door.

For every left/right edge state, rows `4–15` copy the matching approved Cabinet
V3 worktop RGB byte-for-byte and then reapply the exact V41 alpha. This removes
the rejected black strip and the intermediate cyan-cap mismatch. The Broken
handle alone uses Frank's authorised local change: its right end hangs at a
steeper angle.

## Behaviour, healing and motion invariants

After normalising only the immutable set name and ready message, the complete
V50 workbench JavaScript is byte-identical to V46. It therefore preserves:

- exact bay origins, room layout, anchors, pivots and collision geometry;
- drag, snap, swap, Broken movement lock and repair unlock;
- state transitions, animation timing and asset-local effect ownership;
- countertop-healing rules and all four adjacency variants;
- V46 Off/Subtle/Emphatic broken-appliance tremor, component layers,
  independently anchored effects, SFX toggle/replacement and synchronous
  repair shutdown.

No source, approved asset, current selector, production file, gameplay logic or
runtime controller is changed.

## Delivery resilience

All review media is content-addressed under `review-archive/objects`; public
contains only the HTML shell. Every image URL is root-absolute through the
same-origin archive route, so Vercel trailing-slash canonicalisation cannot
rebase an `assets/...` URL into the wrong directory. All unique images preload
and decode before interaction or playback is enabled.
