# Kitchen Sink Worktop Return and Basin Depth V17

Date: 19 October 2026

Status: reversible visual-review draft; unapproved and unpromoted

Review route: `/keeper-review/kitchen-sink-basin-depth-v17/`

V16 established the correct cabinet front: one complete standalone sink unit
whose 84 × 74-pixel structural shell is the literal approved V11 right-hand
bay. The remaining join was too flush. Its countertop did not carry the slight
leftward projection visible where the approved cabinet worktop meets another
unit, and the simplified basin had lost its shallow depth.

V17 leaves the V11 front elevation pixel-identical. Above that elevation it
adds one declared worktop-only amendment: a stepped left return, one native
pixel wide at the transition and no more than two pixels wide through the slab.
When the standalone sink is placed beside the approved two-cabinet run, this
return overlaps the final two worktop pixels at the join; the cabinet bodies
remain adjacent on their original 84-pixel bay bounds.

The fixture remains an intact copper tap over an inset basin. The basin now
uses a narrower rear rim, angled stepped sides, a darker recessed bowl and a
wider brighter front lip, retaining the shallow parallel-projection depth of
the approved cabinetry. No front, drawer, door, rail or plinth pixel is
redrawn.

Standard, On base and Broken base are byte-identical. The corrected On stream
and approved Broken upward-basin-spout animation are copied byte-for-byte from
V16/V15. They remain separate transparent 40-frame, 4-fps, ten-second strips
with the named SFX placeholders `kitchen_sink_water_gentle_loop` and
`kitchen_sink_pipe_burst_loop`.

The verifier proves the exact V11 source crop, the unchanged front elevation,
the two-pixel maximum worktop return and its restricted vertical region, the
basin widening and shading toward the player, the identical static bases, the
unchanged animation hashes and the content-addressed review archive. V11, V16,
Final and runtime remain unchanged.
