# Questions and ideas for Frank (live list)

1. **Resolved — camera.** The art contract provides fixed ROOM, TOWER and
   ISLAND levels. Ordinary vertical travel is off-stage behind the room door;
   the camera never follows visible stairs.
2. **Resolved — Day 1 and expansion.** Day 1 contains kitchen, living room,
   bedroom and lamp room. The lamp room stays topmost, the bedroom stays
   directly below it, and later middle floors arrive through missions in saved
   non-linear order. The old option to buy the lamp room or bedroom is retired.
3. **Mini games inside the game** (V2): fishing (timing/reeling), cooking (follow the recipe steps). Do these replace the automatic action, or sit alongside it as a bonus for extra score?
4. **Resolved — art direction (superseded 26 October 2026).** Pixel art was
   tested and rejected as too blocky when zoomed. New graphics use the bright,
   clean non-pixel flat orthographic direction and exact 1536 × 512 master grid
   in `docs/CODEX_BRIEF.md`. Existing Pixel/runtime assets remain compatibility
   evidence until separately migrated.
