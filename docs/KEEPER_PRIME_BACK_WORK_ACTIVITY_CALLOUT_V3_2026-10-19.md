# Prime Keeper rear-work activity vocabulary V3

**Status:** immutable, reversible review candidate built 19 October 2026;
awaiting Frank's review. Not approved, promoted, deployed or connected to
runtime recipes.

## Outcome

V3 retains the V1 24-frame Prime rear-work body, all approved walk/stop/turn
authorities and the complete route byte-for-byte through V2. It expands only
the separately controlled activity-callout system. The review presentation no
longer draws a counter or any other contextual work surface, and both callout
treatments sit higher with a mechanically verified clear gap above the cap.

The V2 game-language direction remains the visual basis: illustrated tactile
objects with deep navy contours, cream/navy ceramics, sea-glass water, copper,
brass and restrained gold. V3 adds 52 new transparent components to the exact
16 inherited V2 components. Every new component is a separately hash-pinned
256 × 256 cell with at least 16 transparent pixels to every edge and exactly
one declared owner.

## Authored recipes

All phrases are 42 frames at 8 fps, in both the open radial and activity-bustle
treatments, with a five-frame quiet tail. The broad vocabulary is not treated
as proof that one body performance suits every activity.

Rear-work compatible:

- breakfast, lunch, beef dinner and lamb dinner;
- sandwich snack, where bread, ham, lettuce and tomato appear separately and
  resolve into the completed sandwich;
- cake mixing with flour;
- washing up with washing-up liquid;
- tea with kettle, mug and milk;
- lighthouse-lamp polishing;
- beef or lamb BBQ, conditional on a real contextual BBQ asset.

Dedicated pose required before any body review:

- brushing teeth, welding, putting on a vinyl record, showering and sleeping;
- drums, guitar and piano;
- picking fruit or vegetables;
- feeding aquarium fish and cleaning the tank;
- watering plants and sowing seeds.

The dedicated-pose entries are useful, independently reviewable callout
vocabulary only. The review page intentionally withholds the generic rear-work
body for them rather than implying a false animation fit.

## Narrative mappings

The food phrases preserve explicit relationships requested by Frank:

- pig resolves to bacon at breakfast;
- cow resolves to steak in beef dinner and BBQ phrases;
- sheep resolves to lamb chops or a leg of lamb;
- sandwich ingredients resolve to the finished sandwich hero image.

Sleep adds a sheep jumping a fence. Music adds fictional era/archetype
performers—a headband-and-vest rock drummer, a psychedelic left-handed
guitarist and a powdered-hair classical composer—without using an exact real
person's likeness. Aquarium phrases add a castle, plants/gravel, treasure chest
and aerator so the activity reads as tank care rather than an isolated fish.

## Deterministic isolation and ownership

Thirteen selected 1536 × 1024 source sheets each declare one explicit vertical
and horizontal ownership line. The builder rejects alpha on either shared
boundary, then registers the four disjoint cells independently. One earlier
story-figure sheet is retained as rejected provenance because no transparent
horizontal ownership line existed; it contributes no component.

The component atlas preserves the same 256-pixel cell geometry. The verifier
checks every component and every atlas cell for edge alpha, transparent gutter,
hash, owner count and source partition. It also checks all 25 phrase timelines,
the sandwich-resolution order, animal lineage, cap clearance, absence of
counter/furniture markup, independent SFX declarations and exact V2 body/route
inheritance.

## Approval and recovery boundary

V3 requests visual review of vocabulary, recipe storytelling, raised placement
and the no-counter presentation only. It does not change or re-request approval
for the Prime body or route, and it does not promote runtime assets, edit the
animation Workbench, modify Kitchen work or deploy. Rejection is reversible by
discarding the V3 draft/page/archive set and only its new content-addressed
objects.
