# Prime Keeper rear-work activity callouts V2

**Status:** immutable, reversible callout-only review revision built 19 October 2026; awaiting Frank's visual review. Not approved, promoted, deployed or connected to runtime recipes.

## Why V2 exists

Frank rejected both V1 callout evidence treatments because their flat, generic symbols did not belong to the game's design language. V2 preserves the useful V1 foundation—the 24-frame Prime back-to-camera body and its complete approved route—without changing one body or route byte. It replaces only the activity-callout artwork, composition and in-context evidence.

Frank's supplied image is directional rather than an art authority. V2 takes only its radial energy, varied scale and scattered movement. It copies no character, question mark, symbol, pixel or exact linework. The approved Prime master and current Kitchen object finish guide the deep navy contours, cream/navy ceramics, sea-glass water, copper metal, restrained gold and smooth material shading.

## Review candidates

Four independently selectable vocabularies are provided:

- cooking: copper pan, wooden spoon, cream/gold steam and seasoning;
- mixing: cream/navy bowl, whisk, egg and flour puff;
- washing: cream/navy plate, gold sponge, sea-glass droplets and cutlery;
- generic repair: copper/steel tools, fastener, oil can and sparks.

Each has two equal 42-frame / 5.25-second treatments:

1. **Open radial orbit:** no bubble; the hero object anchors a restrained arc of smaller supporting objects and activity marks.
2. **Activity bustle / bubble:** a closer cluster led by that activity's own steam, flour, water or sparks rather than a generic UI speech balloon.

Neither treatment is preferred or approved. Both finish with five metadata-quiet frames. Body, activity cloud/marks, primary object, supporting objects, rear furniture and contextual SFX declarations are independently controllable. Nothing is baked into the Keeper.

## Immutable inheritance and isolation

V2 hash-pins the complete V1 catalogue and batch register. Its `body` and `route` records are structurally identical to V1, so the action still receives the exact approved rear endpoint after walk → stop → turn and returns the same bytes before the reverse turn and departure.

The four generated 1536×1024 source sheets are retained in the V2 draft. Each is divided by an explicit x/y ownership partition into four non-overlapping source cells. Sixteen components are then registered to separate 256×256 transparent cells. The deterministic verifier rejects edge alpha, gutters below 16 pixels, unknown or cross-activity component ownership, changed hashes, malformed phrases, missing quiet tails, changed V1 body/route data, protected-file mutation or any approval/promotion flag.

Both contact sheets show the sampled callout frames over the actual inherited Prime body and separate rear furniture at game scale. The isolated callout pane remains available in the review shell for close inspection, but it is no longer the only evidence.

## Approval boundary

V2 asks only whether either callout treatment now fits the game and communicates the four activities clearly. It does not change or re-request approval for V1's body motion, approve SFX, promote runtime assets, update the animation workbench, alter Kitchen work or deploy. Rejection is reversible by discarding the V2 directory, page and review-archive set/object additions alone.
