# How the early Sims shaped this

- Needs that fall over time and that you meet through objects: hunger, energy, fun, hygiene, bladder, social, room (tidiness).
- Mood is the combined state of the needs; the worst one matters most.
- Objects have pop-up menus of actions; the character queues jobs and refuses when grumpy, busy or in need.
- Free will: left alone, he looks after himself.
- Simplified here: one controllable character, no skills or careers, a daily score and credit allowance instead of a job.
