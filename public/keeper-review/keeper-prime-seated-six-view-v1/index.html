Keeper Prime seated six-view V1

Foundation draft · no animation or production promotion

Six seated endpoints derived from Prime Keeper: direct front, direct back, either profile and rear three-quarter to either side. Approving these locks the targets for the stand ↔ sit animations.

Look for: one Keeper identity, equal registered height, stable cap/head size, the same rotund body, flat boots, and believable contact with the 11-lp seat line. The angled views are deliberately rear three-quarter—the useful view for TVs, games and furniture-facing actions.
