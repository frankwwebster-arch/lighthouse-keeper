# Kitchen V10 image-reference prompts

Built-in image generation was used only to prepare high-resolution visual
references. The deterministic builder fits the selected cutouts into the exact
21-lp cells and writes separate reversible draft outputs.

## Refrigerator extraction

Use case: background-extraction. Isolate the exact small white under-counter
refrigerator from the approved family reference. Preserve the bright warm-white
enamel, rounded upper lip, navy outline, brass handle and hinges, lower vent,
feet, shading and polish. One refrigerator only, straight-on, on genuine alpha.
No card, wall, floor, shelf, countertop, text or extra object. The top must be
smooth and continuous; do not flatten or redesign it.

## Shelf study

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one polished, straight-on shallow wooden wall
shelf in the approved kitchen style, with two fitted supports, one compact
trailing plant in terracotta, one modest blue ceramic pitcher, and two small
cream pantry jars. It must fit one 21-lp bay after deterministic scaling. Use
genuine alpha, a perfectly horizontal shelf and coherent contact shadows. No
wall, card, floor, refrigerator, labels, perspective angle or floating props.
