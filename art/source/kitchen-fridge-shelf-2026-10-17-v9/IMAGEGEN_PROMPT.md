# Kitchen V9 fridge and shelf reference study

Built-in `image_gen` was used on 17 October 2026 for a reference-only visual
study. The deterministic production draft is generated by
`scripts/build_kitchen_fridge_shelf_v9.py`; no generated study pixels are
copied into the production sprites.

Input roles:

- Image 1: style and composition reference for the wall shelf, trailing plant,
  pots and jars.
- Image 2: the existing V8 refrigerator whose dimensional treatment needed
  improvement.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: reference-only concept study for a
> pixel-art game asset family. Create a compact visual study for one improved
> under-counter refrigerator and its separate attached wall-detail companion.
> The refrigerator is bright inexpensive white enamel, recognisably a fridge
> for a young child, preserving the brass handle, right-side brass hinges,
> lower navy ventilation grille and dark feet, but restoring dimensional
> polish through a beveled lid, inset door, left-edge highlight, right-edge
> shadow, seams and subtle cool-grey shading; absolutely no brown or wooden
> countertop. The separate shallow wooden wall shelf carries one trailing green
> plant and two or three simple kitchen pots/jars. Use polished high-resolution
> pixel art matching the Lighthouse Keeper family, a straight-on orthographic
> dollshouse view, and keep both elements readable in the same narrow 21-lp bay.
> Show the refrigerator and shelf separately plus one paired arrangement. No
> text, watermark, other furniture, strong perspective, tapering sides,
> V-shaped gaps or photorealism.
