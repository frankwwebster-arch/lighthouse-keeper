# Kitchen V21 image-generation provenance

The built-in OpenAI image-generation tool produced three new transparent,
versioned source images. V20 and Kitchen V5 were used only as visual/function
references; no existing source, raw asset or review page was overwritten.

## Basic oven

`basic-oven-source.png` asks for the exact V20 storm-blue/sea-glass/copper
family in a strict orthographic 21-lp cabinet, but with one visibly narrower
15-lp single-door appliance, compact hob and visible cabinet stiles. The V20
double oven is explicitly retained as Upgrade 1.

## Pedal bin

`pedal-bin-source.png` asks for one simple closed, standalone pedal bin in the
V20 palette, with a compact 10 × 13-lp target and one readable copper pedal.

## Table and chair

`table-chair-source.png` asks for one side-on table and exactly one chair. It
uses the V6 sitting proof as scale authority: 7.75-lp seat support, 8.625-lp
chair depth and 0.5-lp shin clearance. The tabletop target is 14.5 lp above the
floor, 1.5 lp thick and 20 lp wide. The generated objects are deliberately
separated; the deterministic V21 builder normalizes them to those exact values.
