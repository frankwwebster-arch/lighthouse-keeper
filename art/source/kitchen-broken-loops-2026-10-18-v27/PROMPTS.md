# Kitchen V27 generated-source prompts

All images were created with OpenAI's built-in image generation/editing tool in
transparent PNG mode. They are immutable draft inputs, not canonical runtime
art. The V27 builder normalizes them into density-4 cells and adds reusable
effects separately.

Shared direction: precise-object edit; production pixel-art game asset; preserve
the V20/V21 storm-blue fitted cabinet, sea-glass worktop, copper/orange hardware,
navy outline, straight-on view, crisp polished pixels, transparent background;
do not add walls, floors, scenery, text or the effect layer named below.

- Bin: lid forced askew by overflowing rubbish; visibly overfilled. No smell
  lines or flies in the structural base.
- Fridge: top hinge failed; door hangs diagonally from the lower hinge; dark
  interior and a clear electrical-fault point. No smoke, sparks or water.
- Kettle (revised source): lid substantially lifted and tilted to one side with
  a clear dark gap, as if pressure has nearly blown it free. Do not use a body
  crack as the fault. No steam or motion lines.
- Toaster: burnt toast, black scorching and a visibly failed casing. No flames
  or smoke in the structural base.
- Oven: door open about 20% from its bottom hinge with soot around the cavity.
  No smoke in the structural base. V27 composites separate animated black smoke
  and two reusable flame loops on the hob.
- Bowl: mixing bowl smashed into two unmistakable halves with a few contained
  ceramic chips and the spoon displaced.
- Basin: strong crack across the front apron and a visibly failed/bent tap. No
  water in the structural base.
- Table (revised source): one right leg broken but still partly attached at the
  joint, hanging crookedly outward from a splintered connection; table sags to
  that side. No fully detached lower-leg segment.

The initial fully snapped table-leg generation is retained as
`generated/table-broken-snapped-rejected-source.png` so rejection remains
