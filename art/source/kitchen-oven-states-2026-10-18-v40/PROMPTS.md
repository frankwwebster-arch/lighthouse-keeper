## V1 — from-scratch coordinated family

and Broken—from the same construction. Match the approved kitchen's polished
pixel art, dark storm-blue cabinet, turquoise sea-glass worktop, copper
controls, front elevation, outline weight and scale. Off has upright dials, a
dark indicator and a dark empty cavity. On has visibly turned dials, a bright
red on indicator, a warm amber interior light and a simple roasting tin with
generic food. Broken has its door still attached but hanging askew from the
carcass, with a dark damaged cavity and restrained scorch marks. Keep the hob
and body static. Put the three states side by side on transparent background
with generous gutters. No kitchen room, duplicate oven, smoke, flames, sparks,
glow outside the On cavity, animation frames, labels or text.
