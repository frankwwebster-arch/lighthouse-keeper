# Kitchen Rebuild V1 design record

This is the clean-slate standard-state foundation requested after rejection of
the asset-by-asset V32–V41 approach. All non-bin furniture was drawn together
as one coherent panoramic master. The approved bin is the sole retained object
family.

## Binding registration

- room: 193 × 56 logical pixels, rendered at 4 px/lp = 772 × 224 px;
- fitted run: x=84–588, six equal 84-pixel bays;
- order: fridge, kettle, toaster, oven/hob, mixing bowl, basin;
- worktop top: y=134;
- fascia top: y=151;
- rear-wall foot line: y=208;
- visible fitted-run base/shadow ends before y=218;
- table/chair cell: x=680–764/y=148–222;
- approved 72 × 72 bin cell origin: x=8/y=149.

The high-resolution master is normalized in three vertical bands. This keeps
the small countertop objects readable while binding the counter and cabinet
body to the established low fitted-galley proportions. Horizontal normalization
maps the whole unified run to exactly 504 pixels; the six construction bays are
therefore exactly 84 pixels each.

The isolated standard modules are literal 84 × 120 crops of the final
transparent furniture layer. They are evidence of the shared construction,
not independently redrawn or superimposed assets.

## Scope

This gate contains only the standard kitchen. It creates no On or Broken art,
no effects, no sprite strips and no sound cues. If the foundation is approved,
later state families must be constructed from it and must not reintroduce old
V32–V41 pixels.
