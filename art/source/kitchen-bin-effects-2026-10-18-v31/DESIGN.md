# Kitchen bin V31 comic-readable stink effect

V31 preserves the approved V28 bin and room PNGs and the established ten-second
cadence. It replaces V30's object-shaped plume with wordless comic visual
grammar intended to be immediately readable by a young player.

- Three open curling green smell trails rise from the rubbish.
- Four broken translucent haze patches sit behind, never forming one solid
  smoke object.
- Three flies have 2 × 2 px dark bodies, visible paired wings, intermittent
  motion ticks and slow uneven ten-second orbits.
- Effect cell: 80 × 80 px with a transparent safety gutter.
- Playback: 40 frames at 4 fps, one seamless 10-second loop.
- The bin object and rubbish spill remain static and byte-identical to V28.

The deterministic source is `scripts/build_kitchen_bin_effects_v31.py`.
