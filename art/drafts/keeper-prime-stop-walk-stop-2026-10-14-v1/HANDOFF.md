# Keeper Prime stop → walk → stop V1

Review draft only. Nothing here is Final or production.

The route starts on the exact approved Side-right neutral, accelerates through three new bridge poses into exact approved Walk V6 F1, runs two approved V6 walk cycles, then applies the same bridge poses in exact reverse while continuing to move forward and returns to the byte-identical neutral.

Root motion is deliberately separate from sprite pixels. At 8 fps the accelerating displacement deltas are 2, 6, 12 and 18 high-resolution pixels before the approved 21 px/frame walk cadence. Deceleration uses 18, 12, 6 and 2 px. There are zero gait frames with zero displacement.

Rollback is deletion of this directory and `public/keeper-review/keeper-prime-stop-walk-stop-v1` only.
