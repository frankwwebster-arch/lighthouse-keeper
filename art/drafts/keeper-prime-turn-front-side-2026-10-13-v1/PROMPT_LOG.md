# Prompt and generation log

- Built-in image generation was used with the exact approved high-resolution front and Side-right V1 masters as references.
- Six selected full-body transparent poses were generated independently for 8°, 15°, 30°, 45°, 60° and 75° turns.
- Every prompt required the adult restrained face, compact solid-dark rectangular eyes, separate moustache, readable closed mouth, established beard mass, distinct rear hair, one broad cream uniform stripe, coherent full-body rotation and side-turning boots.
- Every prompt rejected smiles, grins, eye glints, round childlike eyes, double stripes, beard/hair confusion, front-facing feet, squash, stretch, bounce, translation, text and scenery.
- The first 15° and 30° outputs foreshortened too quickly. Both were redrawn with the approved front and neighbouring angle as references, then a new near-front bridge was inserted after contact-sheet inspection. Only those selected redraws are retained in this versioned pack.
- Python performs deterministic alpha cleanup, uniform Lanczos registration, strip assembly and evidence rendering only. It does not redraw or warp the Keeper.
