# Keeper Prime Darts Context V8

V8 preserves all 181 V7 Keeper/action frames and route mechanics. It replaces the simplified CSS dart with one smooth red-flight/dark-barrel/silver-point PNG used for both flight and impact, interpolates from the release hand to the board, and makes the final airborne and embedded positions exact. The placeholder rim becomes a segmented traditional board drawn at an acute edge-on angle. Nothing is approved or promoted.
