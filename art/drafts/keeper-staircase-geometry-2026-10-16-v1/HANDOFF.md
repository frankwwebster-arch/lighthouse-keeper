# Keeper staircase geometry V1

This pack preserves the exact staircase used to approve Stair Ascent V16 and
to construct Stair Descent V1. The geometry—not the grey test rendering—is now
an explicit authority: fourteen risers, 7-lp treads, 30/7-lp riser height,
98-lp flight run, 60-lp flight rise, 60-lp finished-floor pitch and a 105-lp
reserved stair core. Every tread rectangle and contact centre is recorded.

The SVG is immutable evidence of the deliberately plain test staircase. It is
not proposed production scenery. A later versioned scenery candidate must add
two newel posts, regularly spaced foreground balusters and one continuous
handrail. That artwork may plausibly occlude parts of the Keeper's boots and
lower legs, but it may not alter a tread, riser, landing/root datum or Keeper
contact coordinate.

The opposite zig-zag flight is a horizontal layout mirror of the same
geometry, not a differently sized staircase.
