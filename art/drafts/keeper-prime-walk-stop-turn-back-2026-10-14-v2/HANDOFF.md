# Keeper Prime walk → stop → turn away V2

V2 is a delivery-only correction of V1. It uses the same approved Walk V6,
stop bridge, side-to-back V9 frames, Fast Timing V1 schedule, root motion and
endpoints. No Keeper pixel has been redrawn.

The V1 page began playback before its externally archived frames had loaded.
At 10–16 fps this could leave an image blank or stale while a redirect and
download completed. V2 gives every published asset a new versioned URL and
preloads and decodes all animation frames before enabling controls or starting
playback.

The review remains draft-only. It does not alter Workbench Final, production
sprites or the runtime manifest.
