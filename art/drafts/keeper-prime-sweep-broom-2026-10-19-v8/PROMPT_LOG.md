# Prime Keeper sweep broom V8 provenance

V8 uses no new generative-image call. It is a deterministic correction built
from the hash-pinned approved Walk V6, stop/walk V1, side-right neutral and
Turn V9 authorities, plus the already preserved Sweep-3 grip studies.

The movement correction is mechanical: exact authority files, exact gait
order, exact frame durations and exact root-motion deltas. Candidate pixels
may differ only inside the saved hard held-arm/hand masks. Transparent or
black extraction-matte pixels are excluded from those masks so they cannot
erase the approved Keeper. The broom remains a separately owned provisional
prop and is not presented for artwork approval in this gate.
