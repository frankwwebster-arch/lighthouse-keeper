# Keeper Timing Lab V1

Timing-only review platform. Approved artwork bytes, frame order, root motion and endpoint contracts remain unchanged.

The initial route is walk → stop → turn to camera V1. Its reviewed baseline keeps every phase at 8 fps. The recommended proposal changes only the approved turn to 10 fps—a 20% shorter turn—while preserving the walk, deceleration, 125 ms neutral settle and front hold.

The browser tool supports independent phase timing, baseline/proposal comparison, section loops, frame stepping, presets, device-local saved proposals, project defaults and JSON export. Final and production remain unchanged.
