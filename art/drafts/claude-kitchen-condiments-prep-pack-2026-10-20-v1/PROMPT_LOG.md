# Claude Kitchen condiments and preparation pack V1 prompt log

**Tool:** built-in image generation, 20 October 2026.

The accepted outputs used the existing BBQ food contact sheet and Kitchen
preparation source as style-only references. The pack records each original
generated PNG SHA-256 and geometry, proportionally fits its visible alpha inside
a 384 × 384 transparent canvas, and stores that review source as lossless WebP.
Final 256 × 256 PNG normalization is deterministic. No generative edit follows
source acceptance.
