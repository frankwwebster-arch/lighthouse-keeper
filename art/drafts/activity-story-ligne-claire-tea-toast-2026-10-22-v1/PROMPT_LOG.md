# Prompt log — tea and toast V1

Built-in image editing was used on 22 October 2026, one picture at a time.
For every call the first input was the exact current semantic/composition
authority. Approved tomato, plate and tool chest components were style
calibrations only; the corrected condiment pack was the material authority for
glass. The generated source was then reduced to the recorded fixed palette by
the deterministic pack builder. No generated gradient survives in a candidate.

## Binding direction

> one even line weight everywhere; a line only where there is a real edge (no
> hatching, no texture strokes); every area closed and filled with one flat
> colour (no gradients, shine or glow; at most one flat shadow tone, rarely);
> the object’s own colour under even daylight; objects drawn accurately like
> Hergé’s cars and machines, simplified by leaving detail out, never by making
> them cute; clarity at 64 px; black only where the thing is black; marks drawn
> in the same line.
