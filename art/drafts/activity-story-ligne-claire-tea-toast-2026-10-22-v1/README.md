# Bubble stories ligne-claire tea and toast V1

**Status:** immutable review gate; six proposed V2s are unregistered

This pack continues the approved Bubble-story action-imagery programme with
the six legacy tea-and-toast pictures that still needed translation:

- `tea-kettle`, `tea-milk`, `tea-mug`
- `toast-butter-jam`, `toast-slices`, `toast-toaster`

The newly commissioned `tea-bag` V1 already uses the line-clear language and
is deliberately untouched. Each candidate preserves the current V1 subject,
count, construction, viewpoint, overlap and broad silhouette. The binding
taxonomy is Frank's eight-rule direction: one even black outline; a line only
at a real edge; every closed area filled with one flat colour; no gradient,
shine or glow; object colour under even daylight; accurate adult construction
rather than cute simplification; clarity at 64 px; and pictorial marks drawn
with the same line discipline.

The production components are deterministic 256 × 256 RGBA derivatives with
fixed palettes, genuine transparency, at least 20 px clear gutter, one semantic
owner and pinned source/current hashes. The five-column contact sheet compares
current V1, proposed V2 on cream and dark, and both at 64 px.

No picture-library selector, story definition, runtime file, approved art or
production animation changed. Stop at this contact-sheet gate. Only an explicit
approval of the exact sheet authorises adding these files as immutable V2s.
