# Keeper Prime seated six-view V1 prompt and decision log

Built-in image editing was used for four project-bound transparent sources.
The two left-facing views are exact deterministic mirrors, not separate image
generations.

## Shared constraints

- One isolated seated Keeper, no chair, prop, floor, shadow or contact sheet.
- Prime Keeper is the absolute identity, proportion, palette and smooth-render
  authority; historical seated art supplies pose mechanics only.
- Adult rotund body; one cream stripe; solid dark rectangular eye(s) without
  glints; separate moustache; readable closed mouth; established beard mass;
  restrained neutral expression.
- Hips on an invisible seat, knees bent, boots flat, hands resting naturally,
  complete silhouette and generous transparent gutter.
- No grin, round/childlike eye, extra limb, crop, pixelated edge or colour
  drift.

## Front

Draw Prime Keeper seated directly facing the camera, symmetrical, with thighs
projecting forward and both boots flat and level. Use the final fully seated
pose in the historical six-frame front strip only as pose mechanics.

## Back

Draw Prime Keeper seated at exactly 180 degrees to camera. Preserve the
approved rear cap, distinct rear hair/beard construction, one jumper stripe,
palette and rear-facing boot geometry. Do not introduce forward toe caps.

## Side-right

Draw Prime Keeper seated in strict 90-degree right profile, with thigh roughly
horizontal, knee near 90 degrees, both boots flat and hands resting on thighs.
Preserve the approved profile head, beard, cap and badge.

## Rear-right

Draw Prime Keeper seated at a stable 45-degree rear-right angle, between direct
back and profile, leaving a clear sightline toward an activity placed to his
right. Preserve correct rotated cap/badge, rear hair distinct from forward
beard and rear-three-quarter boot orientation.

## Deterministic finishing

Source alpha at or below 16 is discarded to remove image-generator fringe
noise. Each non-mirrored source is alpha-cropped and registered to a 264-pixel
visible height in a 256 × 320 cell with Lanczos resampling, then centred and
placed on y=315. The left views are exact horizontal mirrors of the completed
registered right views.
