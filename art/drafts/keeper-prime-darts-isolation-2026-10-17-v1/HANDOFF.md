# Darts cell-isolation candidate V1

This corrective draft rebuilds the fourteen generated core poses from whole-source connected components rather than rectangular 7×2 crops. It recovers actor pixels crossing nominal source boundaries, removes foreign neighbouring-actor fragments, and excludes four detached projectile components because Darts V3 renders flying darts as independent route effects. Registered F01–F08 and F23–F30 are byte-identical to immutable V2. No palette correction, approval or promotion is included.
