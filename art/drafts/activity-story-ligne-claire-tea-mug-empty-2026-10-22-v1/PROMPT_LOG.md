# Prompt log — empty tea mug V1

The built-in image editor received the exact approved filled mug and this
instruction on 22 October 2026:

> Make an empty version of this exact enamel mug. Change only one thing: remove
> the warm-brown tea contents and reveal the empty inside bottom as one flat
> warm-cream enamel area. Preserve the complete silhouette, viewpoint, handle,
> rim, outlines, colours, centring, transparent gutter and antialiasing. Add no
> shading, reflection, inner contour, steam, spoon, saucer, text or extra object.

That result was rejected: it redrew the approved mug and introduced tonal
modelling. The accepted candidate instead replaces only source RGB `#a65f2a`
with existing enamel RGB `#f6e5b7`, preserving alpha and every other RGBA byte.
