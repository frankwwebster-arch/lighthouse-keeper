# Keeper Prime Walk V5

Review draft only. Nothing in this directory is production or Final.

V5 treats the accepted V4 legs as the timing authority and derives one complete contralateral arm cycle from them. F1 and F5 remain byte-identical endpoint poses. F2-F4 and F6-F8 amend only declared arm and immediate occlusion regions.

The intended arm sequence is: near arm back at F1, passing at F3, forward at F5, passing at F7, and back again at the loop seam. The far arm is always opposite.

Rollback is deletion of this V5 directory and `public/keeper-review/keeper-prime-walk-v5` only.
