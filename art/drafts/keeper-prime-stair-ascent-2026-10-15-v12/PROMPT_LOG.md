# Keeper Prime stair ascent V12 prompt and decision log

Built-in image generation was used for isolated arm studies only. Two initial studies produced essentially the same forward-arm silhouette; the second is retained as rejected source provenance. A targeted opposite-phase rerun required the large foreground arm behind the torso at screen-right and the smaller background arm forward at screen-left.

The deterministic preparation script registers each study to the V7 contact authority and admits generated pixels only within two feathered arm polygons. Generated head, face, beard, central torso, trousers, legs and boots are excluded. The contact legs come from the V6 pose-4 sources through the same contact-lock method as V7, retargeted to a 56-source-pixel/7-lp horizontal separation while retaining the proven 34-source-pixel/60÷14-lp vertical separation.

Prompt invariants for all studies: exact Prime identity and palette; screen-left facing; transparent background; exactly two connected arms and hands; no scenery, stairs, text, shadows, duplicate limbs or changed leg geometry. The selected studies are preserved in `sources/generated/`; all generated source SHA-256 values are recorded in `source-preparation-v12.json`.
