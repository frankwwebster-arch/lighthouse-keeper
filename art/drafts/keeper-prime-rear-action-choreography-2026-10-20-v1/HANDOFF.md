# Prime rear-action choreography V1 handoff

This immutable, reversible review candidate keeps the approved Prime Rear
Action Sequence V1 and Activity Cloud Composer V6 authority bytes unchanged.
It adds four richer callout stories—Garden BBQ, sandwich assembly, washing up
and general repair—each with independently authored open and bubble staging,
explicit 42-frame full/reduced-motion data, separate first-class comic-symbol
components, declared contextual SFX cues and a five-frame settled quiet tail.

The review can independently hide the body, hero/result, supporting objects,
symbols, treatment marks and SFX declaration. It can switch treatment, speed,
looping and reduced motion, and exposes both path overlays and exact per-frame
transform data. All image URLs decode before the controls unlock or playback
starts.

Nothing is approved, promoted, deployed or connected to runtime. Kitchen,
Final assets and all prior immutable review directories remain unchanged. The
candidate is discarded by removing only the files listed in its batch
register and its one content-addressed review set.
