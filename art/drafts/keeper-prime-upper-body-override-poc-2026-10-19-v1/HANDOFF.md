# Prime Keeper upper-body override POC V1

This is not a broom animation. It tests one reusable `hold_shaft_two_hand` profile through the approved Walk V6 gait phases and Turn V9 angles. The original swinging arms are replaced, not overlaid. This is deliberately a clean full-character motion proxy, not pixel-identical production art; the prop layer is empty. Nothing is approved, promoted or wired into runtime.
