# Keeper Prime core transitions V2 — image-generation prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen`, identity-preserving edit workflow. All three outputs
were requested as six isolated full-body poses on genuine transparency. The V1
strip supplied choreography only; exact registered endpoints supplied binding
identity, scale and geometry.

## Side profile to full face

Redraw one coherent six-pose Keeper Prime turn from the exact right-facing
profile to the exact full-face canon: profile, shallow three-quarter,
mid-three-quarter, near-front, almost-front, full-front. Use one fixed
orthographic camera and one rendering family. Keep the same short, stocky,
slightly rotund adult, head/body ratio, cap and beard scale, torso width, limb
thickness, boot size, baseline, vertical scale, palette and broad outline.
Frames 2 and 3 must remain especially close in perspective and apparent size.
Preserve solid dark rectangular eyes without glints, a separate moustache, a
readable closed mouth, compact nose and established beard mass. Avoid zoom,
shrinkage, thinning, childlike proportions, smiles, a pixie grin, a mouth merged
into the moustache, soft restyling, coarse pixel blocks, duplicate anatomy,
detached fragments, props, shadows, backgrounds, guides or edge contact.

## Side profile to directly away

Redraw one coherent six-pose Keeper Prime turn from the exact right-facing

especially close in perspective and apparent size. Rear poses need coherent

Avoid zoom, shrinkage, thinning, childlike proportions, soft restyling, coarse

## Side stop to seated profile

Redraw one coherent six-pose side-view Keeper Prime sit from the exact profile
stop: exact upright, knees begin bending, controlled lowering, deeper lowering,
near-seated, settled seated profile. Preserve the accepted V1 choreography and
do not draw the chair. Use one fixed orthographic camera and one rendering
family. Keep the same short, stocky, slightly rotund adult, head/body ratio,
cap and beard scale, torso width, limb thickness, boots, palette and outline.
Frames 2 and 3 must match the first frame's apparent pixel density and model.
Preserve the rectangular unlit eye, separate moustache, readable closed mouth,
compact nose and beard mass. Avoid zoom, sudden shrinkage or thinning, round or
glinting eyes, smiles, a pixie grin, mouth/moustache merging, soft restyling,
