## Shared correction prompt

- Change only the glass/material rendering; preserve the exact object,
  geometry, silhouette, viewpoint, closure, label, contents, count,
  arrangement and scale.
- Glass is mostly unfilled and visually quiet, described by the outer ink
  contour and at most two or three short pale-blue construction ticks.
- Remove every long white reflection band, vertical stripe, glossy highlight,
  reflected colour wedge, luminous rim, bevel, gradient and shaded glass
  volume. Do not replace shine with more texture.
- Use fine lively near-black contour, flat opaque spot colours, one base colour
  plus at most one small darker region per non-glass material, controlled minor
  irregularity and sparse purposeful internal marks.
- Preserve genuine transparency, centring, orientation and generous gutter.
- Avoid glossy product rendering, vector app icons, cartoon stickers, 3D,
  airbrush, painterly modelling, shadows, scenery, text and watermarks.
