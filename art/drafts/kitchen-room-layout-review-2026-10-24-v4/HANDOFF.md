# Kitchen room layout V4 correction handoff

V4 is the active reversible review boundary for corrected loose assets. It does
not alter the approved source master, gameplay, the live 84 x 74 compatibility
runtime, or immutable V1-V3 evidence.

The six fitted units now use one locked 1368 x 284 native base in OFF, ON and
BROKEN. Only the user-directed OFF tap registration repair precedes that lock.
Warning lamps, oven cavity light, declared damage and floor water are separate
transparent overlays; state changes cannot move the cabinet run.

Loose assets use native pixels only. Kettle ON is byte-identical to OFF and has
no drawn steam. The toaster seats toast onto its OFF body. Kettle and bowl
neighbour-state components are excluded, shadows are removed, and declared
contact rows place objects on their supports. The bin body is enlarged by
native-pixel straight-run extension, not scaling. The rear chair and table are
checked against the native 256 x 320 seated Keeper.

outputs and V4 review-set references. Do not delete or regenerate
`fitted-units-static-native.png`, the dishwasher warning layers, oven light or
their approval record: the approved fitted subset remains hash-locked in
`docs/review/kitchen-fitted-units-lock-approval-2026-10-24.json`.

Verify with `npm run verify:kitchen-room-layout-review-v4`.
