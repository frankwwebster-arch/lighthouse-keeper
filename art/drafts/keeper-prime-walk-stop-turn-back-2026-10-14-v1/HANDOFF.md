# Keeper Prime walk → stop → turn away V1

Status: review draft; not Final and not production.

This route introduces no new Keeper drawing. It composes two exact Walk V6
cycles, the approved walk-to-stop bridge, the exact approved Side-right → Back
V9 turn, and the approved Fast Timing V1 transition profile.

The route translates only during walking and deceleration. It reaches the exact
Side-right stop before rotation begins, omits a duplicated stationary settle,
and ends on the exact approved rear neutral. The review endpoint hold is the
625 ms Fast Timing V1 hold.

Approval of this composite route is a separate visual gate. It does not place
the route in Workbench Final or change runtime sprites/manifests.
