# Prime Keeper rear-work and activity-callout V1

This immutable review candidate is the first Prime replacement proposal for the historical `keeper_cook_back` choreography. It is not approved, promoted or deployed.

## What is in the candidate

- A genuine 24-frame direct-rear work performance at 4 fps (six seconds). It uses 21 distinct working poses between exact approved rear-neutral endpoints; it is not an eight-frame block repeated three times.
- Exact byte copies of the approved rear neutral at F1, F23 and F24. The last two frames provide a half-second neutral settle before the approved reverse turn.
- A 72-frame complete route: approved walk, approved deceleration, approved turn away, 24-frame action, byte-reversed approved turn, approved acceleration and approved walk away.
- Independently switchable 42-frame callout phrases at 8 fps for cooking, mixing, washing and generic repair. Mixing and washing are two authored phrases in the shared `mixing-washing` family.
- Equal bubble-free-fan and cloud/puff treatments. Neither is preferred or approved; Claude's note and HTML sketch are hash-pinned as non-authoritative directional references and contribute no art or definitive choices.
- Separate body, envelope, icon, rear-furniture and SFX controls. Furniture sits behind the Keeper. The Keeper body has no prop, icon or SFX baked into it.

## Construction and evidence

The three selected generated grids contribute pose geometry only. Their opaque backgrounds are removed deterministically and every visible candidate RGB value is registered from the exact rear Prime authority. Trousers, boots and the central lower outline are locked from that authority. The initial generated eight-pose proposal is retained as rejected provenance because it was too short and raised the hands too close to the head.

Every body frame has nine disjoint semantic ownership masks. The verifier recomputes ownership pixel by pixel, rejects shared-edge alpha, checks a minimum four-pixel transparent gutter, compares every strip cell with its source frame, checks every visible component against the exact rear palette and proves the V1/V2 rear-colour regression still distinguishes the rejected dark V1 from corrected V2.

The shell preloads and decodes every unique image before enabling playback.

readable draft catalogue and contains only fields required for playback.

## Approval boundary and rollback

No canonical raw art, Final/workbench entry, runtime manifest, production sprite, approved route authority or Kitchen file changed. To reject the candidate, discard this draft directory, the matching review shell/set manifest, its references in `review-archive/index.json`, the object files referenced only by that set, and the three candidate-specific scripts. No production restoration is needed.

The larger conversion of every historical draft animation to Prime remains future animation-bank work. This candidate establishes the rear-work pattern only.
