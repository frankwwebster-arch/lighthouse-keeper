# Kitchen room layout V6 handoff

V6 is the reversible correction for baked loose-asset shadows and furniture
depth. V5 remains frozen as rejected shadowed evidence. The fitted-unit base and
allowed overlays are inherited byte-for-byte from their V4 approval; live
gameplay and the 84 x 74 compatibility runtime are unchanged.

No loose output contains a contact shadow. Countertop, floor and furniture
surfaces remain owned by the room rather than by movable sprites. The table is
centred at x=1824 kitchen-source pixels, exactly pace 16, leaving a 51-pixel
visible gap after the fridge. The rear-facing chair shares that x-anchor and is
registered 24 pixels farther into the foreground.

Depth order is table, approved rear-seated Prime Keeper, chair. The table is
therefore behind the Keeper and the chair is in front, with the Keeper's back
to the camera. Rear-seat proof is enabled by default; the standing scale proof
remains available as an optional control.
