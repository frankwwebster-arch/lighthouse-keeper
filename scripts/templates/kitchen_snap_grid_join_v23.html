Guarantee

The pointer position is never saved. A drop resolves to the nearest legal slot origin after occupancy and footprint checks. The interaction anchor is then snapped body x + body width ÷ 2. For the default sink this is 336 + 84 ÷ 2 = 378, exactly matching the established Prime stop/turn anchor. Exact adjacency produces a zero-pixel gap, so the approved V22 body backfill and seamless countertop cap use their canonical registration.

Two-bay upgrades use the same rule: their start must be a legal grid origin with two contiguous free bays, and their interaction target is the midpoint of the complete 168-pixel body.
